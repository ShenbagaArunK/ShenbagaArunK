### Who I Am

I used to texture 3D worlds — deciding whether a stone wall looks ancient or freshly plastered, whether skin reads alive under light.
It's a discipline entirely about *reading surfaces* and *asking why things look the way they do*.

Turns out, that's also what data work is.

I transitioned into **Data Analyst and Machine Learning** because I got obsessed with a different kind of surface:
the layer between raw numbers and the insight buried inside them.

> *Most people treat data like a report.*
> *I treat it like a story with an unreliable narrator — your job is to figure out what it's* **not** *telling you.*
